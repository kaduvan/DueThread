Turn on Pro

  1. Open DueThread (click its icon in Chrome's toolbar, then Full view).
  2. Open Settings and choose Enter licence key.
  3. Paste the key and choose Activate Pro.

Keep this key. Save it in a note or a password manager, or email it to yourself. You'll need it if you reinstall DueThread or use it on another computer.
